# 🎬 AI or Real — Run of Show (Futures Dream Team)

A live, phone-based crowd game. The big screen shows a photo or a quote; the crowd
votes **AI** or **REAL** on their phones; points for being right *and* fast; a live
leaderboard crowns a winner. Built for ~500 players.

## ✅ Before the service (5-min setup)

1. On the laptop that drives the projector, open the **big-screen link** above.
2. Press **F11** (or green ● full-screen) so it fills the screen. The control bar
   stays at the bottom — that's yours, the crowd is looking at the big art above it.
3. You'll see the **AI or Real?** lobby with a big QR code and a live "players in" counter.
4. Do a 30-second test on your own phone: scan the QR, enter a name, tap **Start** on
   the laptop, vote, then **Reveal**. You should see your vote land.
5. **Press "Reset" (twice) to clear your test** so you start the real game at 0 players.
   *(Reset also wipes everyone if you run it again — only use it between run-throughs.)*

> 💡 Tip: keep the laptop on **stable wifi or ethernet**, not the crowd wifi. The
> phones can be on anything.

## 🎮 How to run it live

Everything is driven by **one button** (or the spacebar). The primary button changes
label to tell you what's next:

| You're in… | Button says | What happens |
|------------|-------------|--------------|
| Lobby | **Start ▶** | Opens Round 1 — the photo/quote appears, timer counts down |
| A question | **Reveal ▶** | Locks it and shows the answer + how the crowd split |
| A reveal | **Next round ▶** | Moves to the next question |
| After Round 10 | **Show leaderboard ▶** | Shows the Top 10 |
| Leaderboard | **Crown winner ▶** | Confetti + winner's name |

**Keyboard shortcuts** (easier than clicking):
- **Spacebar** or **→** = advance (the big button)
- **R** = reveal · **L** = jump to leaderboard · **H** = hide/show the control bar

**Suggested rhythm per round:** read the quote/show the photo, let the countdown run
~15–20s while you banter ("lock it in!"), hit **Reveal**, read the punchy fact on
screen, then **Next**. ~10–14 minutes total for all 10 rounds.

## 🧯 If something goes sideways

- **A phone looks stuck?** Tell them to refresh the page — their score is saved, they'll
  rejoin right where the game is.
- **Screen out of sync?** Refresh the big-screen page; it reconnects to the live state.
- **Whole thing acting up?** Press **Reset** (twice) to return everyone to the lobby and
  restart clean.
- **Wifi struggling?** The game is built to tolerate drops (it retries automatically), but
  if the venue wifi is truly swamped, encourage phones onto mobile data.
- **Worst case fallback:** you can still play "old-school" — show each screen and have the
  crowd shout/raise hands for AI or Real, using the answer key above.

---

## 🔁 Between the two services (if you run it twice)

Just press **Reset** (twice) on the control bar before the second service. That clears
all players and scores and returns to the lobby. Fresh game, same links.
